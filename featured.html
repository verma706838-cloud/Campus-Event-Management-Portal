CodeSprint 24-Hour Hackathon

Build something useful for campus life in 24 hours, with mentors from local tech companies.

</> 24 HOURS · 30 TEAMS · ₹50,000 IN PRIZES

About the event

CodeSprint brings students from every branch together to design and build a working prototype around themes such as campus mobility, waste reduction and accessible learning. Teams present to a panel of faculty and industry judges at the end.

Event schedule

  1. Sat · 9:00 AM Check-in & team verification at the Innovation Hub lobby
  2. Sat · 10:00 AM Opening keynote and problem statements released; hacking begins
  3. Sat · 8:00 PM Mentor round and dinner break
  4. Sun · 10:00 AM Code freeze and project submission
  5. Sun · 11:00 AM Final demos in front of the judging panel
  6. Sun · 1:00 PM Awards ceremony and closing
